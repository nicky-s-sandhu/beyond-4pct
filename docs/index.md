# Beyond the 4% rule

A historical backtest of retirement withdrawal rules, from Bengen's 4% rule to a flexible rule that follows
how retirees actually spend and is tuned for a 2% chance of running out.

- **[Withdrawal plan explorer](dashboard/)** — tune the recommended plan in your browser and see every
  30-year U.S. retirement since 1928 and 5,000 retirements reshuffled from real history.
- **[Following the plan, year by year](following-the-plan.html)** — the January routine, the planned change for every
  age, and two worked retirements.
- **[Full write-up](beyond-the-4pct-rule.html)** — method, results and charts.
- **[Code and data on GitHub](https://github.com/nicky-s-sandhu/beyond-4pct)**

*Not financial advice.*
